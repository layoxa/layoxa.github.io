# AI for oil and gas operations

Remote sites, rotating equipment and crews that are spread thin. We build the AI that sees trouble coming in the sensor data, guides the technician on site, and writes up the work.

## Where it helps

- **Rotating equipment.** Pumps, compressors, turbines and motors: vibration, temperature and pressure drift flagged before a trip.
- **Remote sites.** A technician alone at a wellsite or station, with every manual, procedure and past repair answering by voice.
- **Inspections.** Rounds, permits and inspection records captured by voice and photo, not typed up at the end of the day.
- **Maintenance backlog.** Work orders ranked by risk, so the crew spends its hours where a failure would cost the most.

## What the crew gets

1. **Early warning.** Normal behaviour learned per machine from your historian and SCADA data, read-only, with drift flagged days ahead.
2. **A voice copilot.** Hands-free answers from your procedures and the history of that exact asset.
3. **Paperwork done.** Work orders, inspection logs and handovers written into your maintenance system.

## Built for operations

- **Read-only by default.** It never writes to control systems.
- **Your data stays put.** On your servers, in your cloud, or in-country.
- **Security first.** Single sign-on, least privilege and a full audit trail.
