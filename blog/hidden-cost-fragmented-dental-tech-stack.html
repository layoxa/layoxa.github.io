Records · 8 min

The hidden cost is not another subscription. It is the chart nobody has reconciled.

A practice can own the complete history and still miss the relationship among evidence, narrative, treatment, time, and benefits.

Leyoxa Care Ledger is a product direction pending a bounded acquisition-readiness sample. Examples below describe the reconciliation method, not customer results.

A dental record is usually reviewed one encounter at a time. That is appropriate chairside, but it makes longitudinal gaps hard to see. The finding may be in one field, the patient’s decline in a note, the treatment plan in another table, and the benefit reset years later.

Four correct records can still produce one missing answer

Evidence documents what was observed. Narrative preserves watches, deferrals, and declines. Treatment-plan history records what was presented or started. Time and benefit context determine whether the history deserves attention now. None of those records is wrong; their separation is the problem.

The three gaps worth reconciling

  1. Evidence documented, diagnosis not recorded. This is a record state for a dentist to re-evaluate—not a generated diagnosis.
  2. Diagnosed or presented, never re-presented. A source-linked recommendation aged without a later follow-up record.
  3. Started, never completed. Part of a documented sequence was completed while the remaining sequence went quiet.

Why a source link matters

A generated summary without provenance creates another record to distrust. A useful ledger item should open the exact note, chart, plan, date, and benefit field behind it. The reviewer can verify the state and reject it without changing the PMS.

How to test the thesis

Use one approved practice, one fixed date range, and read-only access. Reconcile the four layers, let a dentist review the returned items, and measure how many survive source verification. If the ledger does not surface meaningful unreconciled care, stop.

The goal is not more software. It is one answer about the value already documented in the practice’s own history.
