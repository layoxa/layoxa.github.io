Dental office brain · 6 min

A broad “AI chief of staff” is a vision. A source-linked ledger is a product.

The narrower starting point can be evaluated, assigned, audited, and stopped without pretending to run the practice.

Leyoxa Care Ledger is a read-only product direction pending a bounded acquisition-readiness sample.

Why the broad framing fails

An office brain that connects phones, marketing, scheduling, payments, insurance, and clinical records sounds comprehensive. It also hides which source is authoritative, who owns a decision, and whether the software is making a clinical inference.

The Care Ledger narrows the job

Read an approved history. Reconcile evidence, narrative, treatment-plan commitment, time, and benefit context. Return three record-gap types with source provenance. Do not diagnose. Do not write to the PMS.

Intelligence becomes a worklist

A useful result is not 164 alerts. It is a triaged queue: 12 for dentist review, 89 attached to the next scheduled visit, 38 for coordinator re-presentation, and 25 held for source verification. The counts are fictional, but the ownership model is the point.

The practice receives an artifact

The fixed-scope review returns an exportable ledger and report, including value scenarios and limitations. That makes the engagement useful even if the broader product thesis does not continue.

Start with one bounded question the record can answer. Earn the right to expand later.

Start with the ledger, not the omniscient assistant.
