One portfolio ledger. Four accountable queues.

The integration team does not receive thousands of undifferentiated charts. The worklist limits dentist time to the smallest clinical slice and routes the rest to existing visits, coordination, or source verification.

Four record layers become one reviewable item.

No single field tells the whole story. The product reconciles the longitudinal relationship among evidence, narrative, commitment, and time.

Evidence

What is clinically documented.

Periodontal charting, recorded findings, restoration age, and missing-tooth history.

Narrative

What the notes remember.

Declines, watches, deferrals, and referrals that were never closed.

Commitment

What the treatment history records.

Presented, accepted, scheduled, completed, or abandoned.

Time + money

Why a record may matter now.

Staleness, benefit-period context, and estimated plan value.

Every conclusion opens back to its source.

The ledger is not a generated summary detached from the chart. The detail view shows the six-site periodontal measurements, note excerpts, procedures, matching fields, and quantified record alignment behind the item.

The output schema prevents diagnosis.

Leyoxa may identify that evidence is documented and a diagnosis entry is absent. It does not determine whether the patient has a condition or what treatment is appropriate.

Required output language

Evidence documented; diagnosis not recorded; dentist should re-evaluate.

ZeroGenerated diagnoses
