Emergclinic · Leyoxa-developed pilot · No longer live

Route urgent dental demand to available care.

Leyoxa co-built and previously operated the Toronto pilot as an equity-partnership venture. The service is no longer live.

The operating problem

Patients needed a faster path than calling clinic after clinic. Practices needed qualified requests—not another unmanaged directory listing.

A two-sided operating workflow

The pilot connected patient intent with clinic capacity and a clear staff handoff.

01

Structured patient request

Capture location, timing, symptoms, and contact details without presenting an automated diagnosis.

02

Availability matching

Route the request using participating clinic coverage and stated same-day capacity.

03

Clinic handoff

Give staff a concise request with enough context to confirm next steps directly.

What the pilot proved

A narrow dental workflow can connect demand and capacity quickly. Scaling it requires repeatable clinic onboarding, verified availability, responsible routing, and local operating ownership.
