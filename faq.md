# Questions

### What is Leyoxa?
Leyoxa is an AI venture studio. It co-builds vertical AI companies with operators who already have customers.

### What is an AI venture studio?
A team that builds new companies rather than client projects. Leyoxa partners with an operator, builds the AI product with them, and takes part of its return in equity, so both sides win only if the company does.

### How is that different from an AI agency?
An agency is paid for hours and leaves when the project ends. Leyoxa is paid partly in equity and stays, because its return depends on the company working.

### Who is a good fit?
A business with paying customers, real industry knowledge, and one operational problem that costs money every week and that software could solve for many businesses like it.

### What does the operator bring?
Customers, industry knowledge, and the willingness to sell the product to businesses like theirs. Distribution is the operator's half of the company.

### How does Leyoxa get paid?
A build fee, usually below market rate, plus equity in the company being built. Terms are set for each partnership.

### Who owns the code and the IP?
The company owns what is built for it. Leyoxa keeps its reusable tooling and licenses it to the company.

### Which industries does Leyoxa work in?
Any industry with a costly, repeated operational problem and an operator who already has customers. The first partnership is Praxis, in dental, and Leyoxa is committed to it there.

### How many partnerships does Leyoxa take on?
A few a year, so each one gets real engineering attention.

### Where is Leyoxa based?
Leyoxa LLC is a Texas company. Its founder, Sepehr Aflatounian, works from Toronto. Partnerships run across the United States and Canada.
