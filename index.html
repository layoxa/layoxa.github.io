Questions

What operators ask first.

What is an AI venture studio?

A team that builds new companies rather than client projects. Leyoxa partners with an operator who already has customers, builds the AI product with them, and takes part of its return in equity, so both sides win only if the company does.

Who is a good fit?

A business with paying customers, real industry knowledge, and one operational problem that costs money every week and that software could solve for many companies like it.

Who owns what gets built?

The company owns what is built for it. Leyoxa keeps its reusable tooling and licenses it to the company, so each partnership starts further ahead than the last.
