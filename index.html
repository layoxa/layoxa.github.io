Method

Four steps.
No shortcuts.

  1. 01

    Simulate

    Build the world first: the floor, the weather, the traffic, the edge cases nobody has seen yet.

  2. 02

    Break

    Run it until it fails, on purpose, thousands of times. Keep every failure.

  3. 03

    Prove

    Turn the runs into evidence a person can read: what it did, what it refused, what it never saw.

  4. 04

    Release

    Let it work alone, inside the limits the evidence supports. Keep watching.
