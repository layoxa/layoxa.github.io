How it works

Inspect. Test.
Hand over.

01 · See every termination

Checked against the drawings, not memory.

Before energization, Verify walks the electrical rooms, on a robot, a cart or in a technician’s hand. Computer vision checks every label against the one‑line diagram and every lug for its torque mark, and the torque log fills the gaps.

02 · Read every test

The outlier a spreadsheet hides.

Insulation resistance, contact resistance, relay tests, IV curves, load‑bank runs. Verify reads the results, checks them against NETA and IEC limits, and flags the reading that passes the limit but not its peers.

03 · Hand over, done

Find it, fix it, prove it.

Under first load, thermal scans catch the joint running hot. The finding goes to the technician’s iPhone with the evidence, gets closed with a photo and a rescan, and lands in the turnover package.

Questions

What commissioning leads ask.

Does it replace our testing technicians?

No. It works alongside your NETA or commissioning crew: it captures, checks and documents, so each technician covers more equipment and nothing is missed.

Is it safe around energized equipment?

It follows the same rules your crew does. Visual inspection happens on de‑energized gear with covers off; thermal scans under load go through IR windows or with qualified staff present, per NFPA 70E.

Do we need a robot?

No. Verify starts on a cart or in a technician’s hand. A robot pays off for repeated sweeps during first load and on large outdoor yards.

Where does site imagery go?

It stays on site by default. Processing runs on edge hardware, with exports only to the commissioning platform you choose.
