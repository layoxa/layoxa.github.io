Questions

In plain terms.

What is an AI venture studio?

A team that builds AI products together with a business that already has customers, and is paid partly in a share of what it builds. We don’t bill hours and leave; we win only if the product works.

What can AI actually do for a business like mine?

Three things, most often. Help more customers find you, including through AI assistants like ChatGPT and Siri. Earn more from the customers you have, by answering every call and following up. And take busywork off your plate, such as scheduling, reminders and reports.

Who owns what gets built?

The company owns what is built for it. Leyoxa keeps its reusable tooling and licenses it to the company, so each partnership starts further ahead than the last.
