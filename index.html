The Care Ledger

Record state is not clinical judgment.

Leyoxa can show that evidence is documented and a diagnosis entry is absent. Only a dentist decides what it means.

The boundary

Evidence documented; diagnosis not recorded; dentist should re-evaluate.

Zero generated diagnoses. Zero chart writes. Every item opens back to its source and stays reviewable until an accountable person resolves it.

Direct answers

01

What can I buy today?

A fixed-fee dental acquisition record review. Leyoxa tests access and chart quality, then returns a source-linked Care Ledger, an owner-assigned worklist, methodology, and stated limitations.

02

What does it cost?

$7,500–$15,000 for a readiness sample; $15,000–$30,000 for one full location. A fixed transaction engagement, not per-seat software. Final scope follows the access review.

03

Does Leyoxa diagnose or write to the PMS?

No to both. The engagement is read-only. A permitted output is: evidence documented, diagnosis not recorded, dentist should re-evaluate.
