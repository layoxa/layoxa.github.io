# Leyoxa

> AI venture studio. Leyoxa co-builds vertical AI companies with operators who already have customers.

You bring customers and a problem worth a company. We build the system that solves it, and take part of our return in equity.

## How we build

1. **Observe.** We work inside a business that already has customers, and watch where the money and the hours leak.
2. **Find the bottleneck.** Many complaints, one constraint. We name the problem that costs money every week.
3. **Build.** Agents, integrations, memory, permissions. Engineered for regulated data from day one.
4. **Deploy.** Into production, through the operator's own customers.
5. **Measure.** Outcomes, not demos.
6. **Own.** When the problem is big enough to be a company, we build it as one, with the operator, and share the upside.
