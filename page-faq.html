FAQ

Direct answers.

01

What does Leyoxa do?

AI strategy, experience design, software engineering, and systems integration for dental practices and dental groups.

02

Do you sell a fixed software product?

No. We work as a services partner. The scope is shaped around your workflows, systems, users, and goals.

03

Can you integrate with our practice systems?

Yes, where the relevant API or approved access method supports the required workflow. We define read/write depth, sync, failure handling, and ownership before implementation.

04

Is the software HIPAA compliant?

Compliance is a shared operating responsibility, not a blanket product label. We define controls, BAA requirements, subprocessors, access, retention, logging, and each party's responsibilities for the engagement.

05

Who leads the work?

Founder Sepehr Aflatounian leads the strategy and technical delivery, with specialist support added when the scope requires it.
