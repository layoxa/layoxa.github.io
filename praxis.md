# Praxis

Praxis is the first company Leyoxa co-built. A dental marketing company had the practices. Leyoxa built the technology.

## The problem

Dental practices lose patients at the front desk. Calls ring out after hours and at lunch, reviews go unanswered, and the owner sees the numbers weeks late.

## All three problems, in one platform

- **More patients find the practice.** The GEO / SEO agent keeps the practice findable in search and in AI answers, and the Reputation agent gathers its reviews from Google, Facebook, Yelp, Healthgrades and other sites and scores each provider.
- **More revenue from every patient.** The Voice agent answers patient calls, books, reschedules and cancels in the practice’s own scheduling system, and hands off to staff when a person is needed. The Smile Simulator lets a prospective patient preview a new smile from a photo on the practice’s website.
- **Less busywork.** The Executive Assistant answers the owner’s questions from the practice’s own data, and Call Coaching reviews calls so the front desk gets better every week.

## How it is built

- **Into the practice’s existing systems.** The agents read and write the practice-management software the office already uses, rather than adding another tool to check.
- **For patient data.** Praxis handles protected health information, so access is limited to named people under business associate agreements.
- **Measured.** Each agent is judged by what it actually did on a call or a record, not by a demo.

## Where it is now

Live with dental practices across the United States. Leyoxa remains Praxis’s technology partner.
