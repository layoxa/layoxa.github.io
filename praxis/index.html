Leyoxa / Praxis: AI agents for dental practices

Praxis

Praxis is the first company Leyoxa co-built. A dental marketing company had the practices. Leyoxa built the technology.

The problem

Dental practices lose patients at the front desk. Calls ring out after hours and at lunch, reviews go unanswered, and the owner sees the numbers weeks late. The marketing company behind Praxis saw it across every practice it served.

What was built

Six AI agents, in production:

  • Voice. Answers patient calls, books, reschedules and cancels in the practice's own scheduling system, and hands off to staff when a person is needed.
  • Reputation. Gathers a practice's reviews from Google, Facebook, Yelp, Healthgrades and other sites, and scores each provider.
  • GEO / SEO. Keeps the practice findable in search and in AI answers.
  • Smile Simulator. Lets a prospective patient preview a new smile from a photo, on the practice's own website.
  • Executive Assistant. Answers the owner's questions from the practice's own data.
  • Call Coaching. Reviews calls so the front desk gets better every week.

How it is built

  • Into the practice's existing systems. The agents read and write the practice-management software the office already uses, rather than adding another tool to check.
  • For patient data. Praxis handles protected health information, so access is limited to named people under business associate agreements.
  • Measured. Each agent is judged by what it actually did on a call or a record, not by a demo.

Where it is now

Live with dental practices across the United States. Leyoxa remains Praxis's technology partner.
