San Antonio: 828 dental offices in Bexar County, and no large employers.

Federal establishment data for the San Antonio metro, read against national DSO consolidation — what the public record supports, and what it does not.

September 3, 2026Leyoxa

Metro screening is the first stage of acquisition intelligence: is the market structured so that an expansion or acquisition thesis deserves deeper work? For San Antonio, federal data provides a useful starting point.

828 dental offices in Bexar County, and almost none of them are large

The Census Bureau's County Business Patterns counts 828 establishments under NAICS 6212, Offices of Dentists, in Bexar County in 2022, employing 5,590 people against an annual payroll of $299.0 million. Neighbouring Comal County adds 76 establishments, 526 employees and $33.0 million.

The size distribution is the part that matters to an acquirer:

  • 341 establishments with fewer than 5 employees
  • 284 with 5 to 9
  • 174 with 10 to 19
  • 29 with 20 to 49
  • none reported at 50 or more

Three quarters of the county's dental offices employ fewer than ten people, and the Census records no large employer at all. That is a market of independent practices rather than one already consolidated behind a small number of operators.

National consolidation is running ahead of this market

The American Dental Association's Health Policy Institute reports that the share of dentists affiliated with a dental support organization more than doubled from 7.2% in 2015 to 16.1% in 2024, and names Texas among a group of states where roughly one quarter of dentists were DSO-affiliated by 2024. The same report records practice ownership falling from 85% of dentists in 2005 to 73% in 2023, concentrated among earlier-career dentists.

These are national and state-level figures. Neither the ADA nor the Census publishes a DSO share for San Antonio specifically, and we do not estimate one.

Demand context for the metro

San Antonio's population was 1,548,422 as of July 2025 within a Bexar County population of 2,160,088. Median household income was $65,056 in the city and $72,341 county-wide, against $101,889 in Comal County — a material payer-mix difference across a single metro. The share of residents under 65 without health insurance was 20.1% in the city and 18.1% in the county.

What this does not tell you

Establishment counts are not practice counts, and none of these sources carries revenue, EBITDA, patient volume, chair count, or valuation multiples for any individual practice. This page describes a market structure. It is not a valuation, not diligence, and not sufficient on its own to justify an acquisition.

Why a fragmented market changes the review

An acquirer buying into a market of sub-ten-employee practices inherits records written by small teams on heterogeneous systems, often with no prior integration event. That is the condition the Care Ledger is built for: many small source histories, each with its own conventions, reconciled into one accountable post-close worklist with every item linked to where it came from.
