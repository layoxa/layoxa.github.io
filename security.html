Leyoxa’s current product direction is deliberately constrained: approved records are read, reconciliation states are returned, and the practice remains responsible for every clinical decision and authoritative PMS entry.

The product boundary is a control.

“No diagnosis” and “no PMS write” are enforced as product requirements, not left to marketing language.
