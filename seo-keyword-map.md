## Cluster architecture

- The commercial pillar `/dental-ai-software.html` links to every solution and
  the most important informational guides.
- Solution pages link down to implementation articles and proof pages.
- Every new article links back to the pillar and sideways only when the reader's
  next question is genuinely related.
- Case studies carry proof intent and link to the relevant service or guide;
  they should not be rewritten to compete with the main commercial keyword.

## Measurement plan

- Submit the updated sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Record current impressions, clicks, average position, and indexed status by URL.
- Review query-to-page alignment after 14, 30, and 60 days.
- Add internal links from pages already earning impressions before publishing
  additional overlapping content.
